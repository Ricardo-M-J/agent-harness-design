# 宣传册设计 SKILL

## 概述

为品牌设计高质量宣传册（Brochure），适用于产品手册、品牌介绍、活动宣传等场景。

## 核心原则

### 1. 朱家角质量对标

**质量特征：**
- **排版**：网格系统对齐，留白呼吸感，信息层级清晰
- **色彩**：品牌色主导，高饱和度但不刺眼，冷暖平衡
- **材质**：特种纸纹理可见，印刷质感真实
- **光影**：自然光或柔光箱，温暖色调，有层次的阴影

### 2. 宣传册类型

| 类型 | 尺寸 | 用途 |
|------|------|------|
| 三折页 | 210x297mm (A4展开) | 活动宣传、产品介绍 |
| 对开页 | 420x297mm (A3) | 品牌手册、企业介绍 |
| 骑马钉手册 | 210x297mm 多页 | 产品目录、服务指南 |
| 精装画册 | 250x250mm 方形 | 高端品牌展示 |

### 3. 内容结构

**封面**：
- 品牌Logo prominently displayed
- 主视觉图形/摄影
- 标题（简洁有力）
- 副标题/Slogan

**内页**：
- 品牌故事/介绍
- 产品/服务展示
- 核心优势/数据
- 联系方式/CTA

## Prompt 模板

### 三折页宣传册

```
Professional brochure design, tri-fold layout, premium print quality.

COVER DESIGN:
- Brand logo "{BRAND_NAME}" prominently at top
- Main headline in bold modern typography
- Hero visual: {main_visual_description}
- Brand colors: primary {PRIMARY_COLOR}, secondary {SECONDARY_COLOR}, accent {ACCENT_COLOR}

INSIDE PANELS:
- Left panel: Brand story with elegant typography
- Center panel: Product/service showcase with high-quality imagery
- Right panel: Key benefits with icons and statistics

DESIGN DETAILS:
- Grid-based layout, clean alignment
- Generous white space for breathing room
- Premium paper texture visible (matte finish)
- Subtle shadows suggesting physical brochure
- Professional photography style lighting
- 4K resolution, print-ready quality

NO: cluttered layout, low-res images, generic stock photos
```

### 对开页品牌手册

```
Premium brand brochure, bi-fold or gate-fold layout, luxury print quality.

FRONT COVER:
- Minimalist design with large brand logo
- Embossed or foil-stamped effect suggestion
- High-end photography or abstract brand visual
- Color: {PRIMARY_COLOR} dominant with {ACCENT_COLOR} highlights

INSIDE SPREAD:
- Full-bleed photography on one side
- Clean typography on the other
- Information hierarchy: headline, subhead, body text
- Data visualization: charts, infographics in brand colors

MATERIAL & FINISH:
- Thick cardstock texture (300gsm suggestion)
- Soft-touch matte laminate
- Spot UV or foil accent details
- Professional studio lighting, subtle shadows
- Ultra high detail, magazine editorial quality
```

## 设计检查清单

```
- [ ] 品牌Logo prominently displayed
- [ ] 品牌色严格遵循 (精确HEX值)
- [ ] 中英文名称正确呈现
- [ ] 网格系统对齐
- [ ] 留白呼吸感充足
- [ ] 材质纹理可见 (纸张/印刷质感)
- [ ] 光影自然温暖
- [ ] 信息层级清晰
- [ ] 4K/印刷级质量
```

## 风格参考

**朱家角风格特征：**
- 明亮温暖的自然光
- 高饱和度但和谐的色彩
- 真实材质纹理 (纸张、布料、陶瓷)
- 轻微生活场景暗示
- 专业但不过度修饰
